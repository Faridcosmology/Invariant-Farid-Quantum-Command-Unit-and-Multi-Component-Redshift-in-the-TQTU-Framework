1. Introduction

Cosmological redshift is ordinarily expressed as a change between emitted and observed photon frequency,

$$ 1+z=\frac{\nu_{\rm em}}{\nu_{\rm obs}}. $$

Within an expanding cosmological model,

$$ 1+z_{\rm cos} = \frac{a(t_{\rm obs})}{a(t_{\rm em})}, $$

where \(a(t)\) is the cosmic scale factor.

The introduction of the Farid Quantum Command Unit creates an additional conceptual requirement. If

$$ E_{\rm FQCU}=h\times1\,{\rm THz}, $$

is intended to provide a universal energy reference, should its underlying \(1\,\mathrm{THz}\) reference frequency itself change when the surrounding plasma or entropy field changes?

The mathematically cleaner answer is no.

The reference frequency is treated as a calibration constant. The physical photon frequency changes during propagation; the calibration frequency does not.

Thus,

$$ \boxed{ h={\rm constant}, \qquad \nu_0=1\,{\rm THz}={\rm constant}, \qquad E_{\rm FQCU}=h\nu_0={\rm constant}. } $$

This distinction separates the ruler from the observable.

2. FQCU Invariance Proposition
Proposition

Assume that the TQTU reference frequency is defined by

$$ \nu_0\equiv10^{12}\ {\rm Hz}, $$

Then

$$ E_{\rm FQCU}=h\nu_0 $$

$$ \boxed{ \frac{dE_{\rm FQCU}}{d\ell}=0. } $$

$$ E_{\rm FQCU}=h\nu_0, $$

$$ dE_{\rm FQCU} = \nu_0\,dh+h\,d\nu_0 =0. $$

$$ \boxed{E_{\rm FQCU}=\text{invariant}.} $$

This is an invariance statement within the definition of the TQTU energy scale, rather than an independently discovered new constant of nature.

$$ \boxed{ 1+z = \frac{\mathcal N_{\rm em}} {\mathcal N_{\rm obs}}. } $$

9. What XRISM Actually Measures

XRISM directly measures X-ray photon energies and spectra.

It does not directly measure the local electron plasma frequency

$$ \omega_p. $$

If the electron density \(n_e\) is inferred from an independent plasma model or spectroscopic diagnostics, one may calculate

$$ \boxed{ \omega_p = \sqrt{ \frac{n_e e^2} {\epsilon_0m_e} }. } $$

Likewise, quantities such as electron temperature, ionization parameter, column density, turbulent velocity, and ionic populations are inferred by fitting physical spectral models.

Therefore the correct data chain is

$$ \boxed{ \text{XRISM spectrum} \rightarrow \text{plasma-model inference} \rightarrow n_e,T_e,\ldots \rightarrow \text{TQTU test}. } $$

This separation prevents an inferred quantity from being mistaken for a directly measured one.
